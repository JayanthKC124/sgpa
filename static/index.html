What is this?

An automatic SGPA calculator for VTU students. Instead of entering every subject and credit manually, you simply upload your marksheet. The system reads it, matches the subjects against the predefined 2022 scheme curriculum, and calculates your SGPA using the official VTU grading system.

How extraction works

When you upload a marksheet (PDF or image), it is read entirely inside your browser using Tesseract.js (open-source OCR). The file is never uploaded to any server or cloud. The extracted text is matched against the predefined 2022 scheme curriculum for your selected semester to find the correct credits automatically.

SGPA Formula

Credit Points = Credits × Grade Point (per subject)

SGPA = Total Credit Points ÷ Total Credits

Subjects with 0 credits (NSS/PE/Yoga) are excluded from the denominator. Failed subjects contribute 0 credit points but their credits still count.
